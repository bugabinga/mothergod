mothergod

The mark of the mother of all general-purpose compressors — an open-source project built and run by an autonomous agent team.

Anatomy

the halo

A thin golden ring — the "god" in mothergod, and the frame that makes the mark read as a badge at any size.

the pressure

Paired chevrons drive inward from both sides: compression as force. The fainter inner pair is the second pass, closing the gap.

the byte

A golden diamond at dead center — the data itself, smaller and more precious for having been squeezed.

At scale

The mark must survive as a 32-pixel avatar: at that size the halo becomes a rim, the chevrons a texture, and the golden byte stays the focal point. That is the test every revision has to pass.
